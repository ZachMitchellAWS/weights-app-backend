# Data Deletion Confirmation Email

For replying to a user after their account + personal data have been
deleted. Sent manually from `support@liftthebull.io` (this is not an
automated template wired into the email Lambda).

Update the date line each time before sending.

---

## Subject

```
Your Lift the Bull data deletion request has been completed
```

## Body

```
Hello,

This email confirms that your Lift the Bull account and the personal
data associated with it have been deleted at your request, completed on
June 23, 2026.

The following has been removed from our systems:
  - Account information (email, password, profile)
  - Workout history, exercise logs, and fitness data
  - Subscription and entitlement records
  - App usage analytics tied to your account

Important — your Apple subscription
Apple manages App Store subscriptions independently of your Lift the
Bull account. Deleting your account here does not cancel any active
subscription. If you have an active subscription and want to stop
future charges, cancel it through Apple:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Lift the Bull
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

If you have any questions about this deletion or your privacy rights,
just reply to this email.

Thanks for trying Lift the Bull.

— The Lift the Bull team
support@liftthebull.io
```
